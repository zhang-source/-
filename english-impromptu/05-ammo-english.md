## 一、玉山藝術品（A1、A7、A11 專用）

> A1「Which artist impressed you the most?」是 2024 年的題目，而且**每年都有藝術題**。
> 選 **一個** 藝術家講深，不要五個都講淺。我建議選**阿曼**或**雷諾瓦**。

### ⭐ 首選：阿曼《音樂的力量》(Arman, "The Power of Music", 1986)

The artist who impressed me the most is **Arman**, a French sculptor.
His work in our head office is called **"The Power of Music."**
He made it in 1986, and it is the largest Arman sculpture in Taiwan.

Here is what it looks like.
He took about **twenty double basses**, cut them apart, and stacked them together into one piece.
One double bass alone is just an instrument. Twenty of them become something else.

That is why this work means so much to E.SUN.
**One plus one becomes more than two.** That is teamwork.

A bank works the same way.
The branch alone cannot serve the customer. The back office alone cannot either.
Only together do we create the full service.

And there is a second meaning. Music heals people.
E.SUN invited the Vienna Boys' Choir and the Twelve Cellists of Berlin to Taiwan,
not to sell anything, but to give people a moment to breathe.
A bank that does that is thinking about more than money.

> **關鍵字**：Arman｜French sculptor｜twenty double basses｜cut apart and stacked｜one plus one becomes more than two
> **可套用**：A1、A7、D1、D3（團隊）

### ⭐ 次選：雷諾瓦 Renoir — "The pain passes, but the beauty remains."（A11 專用）

Renoir was a French Impressionist painter.
He was born into a working-class family — his family were tailors, and they were poor.

But look at what he painted.
Happy women. Flowers. Children. Dinner parties. Warm light.
He lived through a hard time, and he answered it with beauty.

In his last years he had severe **rheumatoid arthritis**.
His hands were deformed. It hurt to hold a brush. He kept painting anyway.
He painted until the final weeks of his life, and he left more than four thousand works.

So when he said **"The pain passes, but the beauty remains,"**
he was not saying something pretty. He was describing his own hands.

What does it mean to me? [停]
It means pain is temporary, but what you create with it is not.
The difficult months I went through are already gone.
What stayed is the person those months made.

> **關鍵字**：Impressionist｜working-class, tailors｜rheumatoid arthritis｜kept painting｜four thousand works
> **可套用**：A11、G6（最大的困難）、A1

## 四、團隊的具體例子（D 類專用 — 抽象論點的解藥）

> D 類題目最容易講成空話。**塞一個奧運故事進去，立刻變具體。**

### 麟洋配 Lee Yang & Wang Chi-lin（羽球雙打金牌）⭐ 最好用

Let me give you a concrete example.
In badminton doubles, our gold medal pair had a very clear division of labor.
One of them said it simply: **"He starts the attack and organizes. I finish and cover."**
Neither of them tried to do everything.

And after they won, they did something I will not forget.
They did not put on their own medals. **They put the medal on each other.**
That is what a team looks like.

### 戴資穎 Tai Tzu-ying（羽球銀牌 — 談運動家精神、面對失敗）

She lost the gold medal match.
Then her opponent ran across the court, held her face, and said:
**"You played very well. Today just was not your day."**

They were rivals for one hour. They were friends the moment it ended.
I think that is what competition should look like.

### 李智凱 Lee Chih-kai（鞍馬銀牌 — 談努力、堅持）

His routine on the pommel horse lasts about **forty-five seconds**.
Behind those forty-five seconds are **nineteen years** of daily practice.
When we see someone succeed, we see the forty-five seconds.
We do not see the nineteen years.

### 郭婞淳 Kuo Hsing-chun（舉重金牌 — 談回饋、Don't prove, improve）

She won gold in weightlifting. Then she donated her prize money to a hospital on an offshore island,
and she funds the weightlifting team at her old school every year.
She also said something I like: after winning bronze in 2016, she stopped comparing herself to others.
**Don't prove. Improve.**

> ⚠️ **2024 巴黎奧運**（題目 D9）我手上沒有素材。你如果要準備這題，
> 請自己補一個 2024 巴黎的印象深刻畫面，再套上上面任何一種講法。

## 六、遇到完全不會的題目怎麼辦（保命流程）

抽到沒準備過的題目，**不要慌，跑這個流程**：

1. **重複題目**（買 10 秒）：
   "That is an interesting question. Let me repeat it — ___. "
2. **承認範圍**（不是道歉，是設定框架）：
   "I will answer this from the angle I know best."
3. **拉回模組**：任何題目都能拉到 M1（玉山）、M2（我自己）、M5（團隊/風險）其中一個
4. **給一個具體例子**（用上面的奧運或藝術品）
5. **用萬用收尾收掉**

> **絕對不要說**："I don't know." / "Sorry, my English is not good."
> 講滿 3 分鐘的爛答案，永遠贏過講 40 秒的好答案。
