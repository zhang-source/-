## 怎麼分辨好答案跟壞答案

| | 好答案（可以學） | 壞答案（不要學） |
|---|---|---|
| 例子 | 玉山自己的數字 | 「世界趨勢」「Overall...」 |
| 長度 | 3 個點，每點有例子 | 5 個點，每點一句空話 |
| 結尾 | 回扣玉山 | "Overall, ... looks bright." |
| 聽起來 | 像一個玉山的人在講 | 像一篇維基百科 |

**評審不是在考你懂不懂 AI，是在考你懂不懂玉山。**

## ⭐ 值得學的：士恆的答案（有具體數字，這才是對的寫法）

### B2 — What are AI applications in E.SUN Bank?（原文，幾乎可直接用）

> AI applications are broadly used in E.SUN bank. Following are advantages of using AI and some examples:
>
> 1. **Reduce the human effort**: E.SUN bank uses AI to recognize bill images, and saves related data into the database. It largely reduces repeated work and saves about **77% of human effort** in the front line.
> 2. **Prevent losses**: To prevent credit card fraud, E.SUN applies AI models combined with large customer data. In theory, E.SUN can determine whether a credit card transaction is fraud in **less than 1 second**. This prevents hundreds of millions in losses.
> 3. **Convenience**: Facial recognition to identify our employees is being developed now.

**為什麼好**：77%、less than 1 second、hundreds of millions —— 三個數字，整段就有重量了。
**小修**：原文 "applicated"→`applies`、"recognized"→`recognize`、"there are more excited development"→`there is more exciting development`。

### C3 — How can we achieve Net Zero in 2050?（原文，數字很好）

> 1. **Using renewable energy**: We have implemented solar power in the **Chiayi branch**, and we can have more green buildings in the future.
> 2. **Digital credit card**: E.SUN published the first digital credit card. It reduces about **1.1 kg of carbon emission** for each card.
> 3. **Responsible lending**: Since most carbon emission in the banking industry comes from **Scope 3**, we can use responsible lending to improve it.

**為什麼好**：嘉義分行、1.1 公斤、Scope 3 —— 具體到不可能是抄來的。
**小修**：`cardon`→`carbon`、`Responsibility credit`→`Responsible lending`、`category 3`→`Scope 3`。

### A7 — What do you learn from the artworks in E.SUN Bank?（原文，很聰明的三段式）

> 1. **Jade Mountain paintings**: different paintings show Jade Mountain in different ways — which implies that we accept different types of talent.
> 2. **Renoir's artwork**: "Pain passes, beauty remains" — which implies that when we go through the downside of life, we gain from the experience.
> 3. **The Jade Mountain Deer**: the first artwork E.SUN collected. Deer are social animals, which implies that people cooperate with each other in E.SUN.

**為什麼好**：每個作品都接一句「所以玉山是…」。這就是藝術題的正確結構 ——
**不是介紹藝術，是用藝術講玉山。**
> 💡 白鹿是「玉山收藏的第一件藝術品」這個點很少人知道，講出來會加分。

## ⚠️ 不要照抄的：明顯是 ChatGPT 的答案

以下四題的答案通篇沒有出現「E.SUN」一次：

| 題目 | 問題 |
|---|---|
| **B5** How can ChatGPT impact our daily life? | Personal assistance / Education / Communication — 三個點都是通用的，跟銀行無關 |
| **F2** Will a recession occur? | 教科書定義，講完沒有結論 |
| **C4** What is the future of renewable energy? | "Overall, the future looks bright." 這種句子是零分句 |
| **F5** How does the pandemic reshape human behaviour? | 結尾還留著 **"Regenerate response"** |

**怎麼救**：這四題的架構可以留，但**每一點後面硬加一句玉山**。

範例 —— B5 從零分變及格：

> ~~"ChatGPT can act as a personal assistant, answering questions and setting reminders."~~
>
> ✅ "ChatGPT can act as a personal assistant. And in a bank, that matters more than it sounds.
> A branch employee spends hours every week on repeated questions.
> If a machine handles those, that employee gets the time back — and spends it on the customer.
> At E.SUN we already use AI this way: our system checks a credit card transaction for fraud
> in **less than one second**. **Technology does not replace people. It gives time back to people.**"

**看出差別了嗎？** 加了玉山的例子、加了數字、加了一句可以收尾的話。

## 給你的結論

學長姐留下的東西裡，**真正值錢的是士恆那三題的數字**，不是那些 ChatGPT 段落。

你要做的事：
1. 把上表的 **77% / less than one second / 1.1 kg / Chiayi / Scope 3 / 133 companies** 背起來
2. 任何 B 類或 C 類題目，**至少丟一個數字進去**
3. 不要學那種「Overall, the future looks bright」的收尾 —— 那是講完等於沒講
