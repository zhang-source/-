# 六大模組 — 背這 6 塊，49 題都能拼出來

> **核心觀念**：3 分鐘不可能為 49 題各寫一篇稿。
> 背 6 個模組，抽到任何題目 → 選 2–3 塊拼起來 → 加上開場與收尾。
>
> **句子刻意寫短。** 短句講起來不容易卡，聽起來也比長句自信。
> 每塊標了語速時間（以每分鐘 110 字的慢速估算，緊張時會更慢，這是保守值）。

---

## 3 分鐘骨架（先背這個，比背內容重要）

| 段落 | 秒數 | 要做什麼 |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 15" | 一個數字、一句引言、或一個畫面 |
| **Position** | 15" | 「我的答案是 ___，有三個原因／兩個面向」 |
| **Point 1 + 例子** | 45" | 模組 A |
| **Point 2 + 例子** | 45" | 模組 B |
| **Counterpoint** | 20" | 「當然，也有人擔心 ___。但我認為 ___」 |
| **Close** | 20" | 回扣 Hook，一句結論 |

**講不滿時的救命句（背熟，可無限拖）：**
- "Let me give you a concrete example."
- "There is one more thing I would like to add."
- "This connects back to what I said at the beginning."

**講太快講完了怎麼辦**：回到 Point 1，說 "Let me go back to my first point for a moment, because I think it matters the most."

## M1 — 玉山 30 秒簡介（約 45 秒｜A、B、C 幾乎全部通用）

> 這塊是**最高投報率**。A 類 13 題有 10 題可以直接開這塊。

E.SUN Bank was founded in 1992.
Our founder had one simple goal — to run **the best bank in Taiwan**.
Three words describe us: **professional, service, and responsible**.

We are not the biggest bank. But we want to be **the most distinctive one**.
Today we have around **thirty locations in ten countries**,
so we can support our customers across Asia, not only in Taiwan.

And we have one rule that every E.SUN employee knows:
**"No business can be considered beyond risk."**
That sentence tells you what kind of bank we are.

> **中文提醒**
> - "founded" 唸 /ˈfaʊndɪd/，不是 "found it"
> - "distinctive" 是玉山的關鍵字，一定要唸清楚：dis-TINC-tive
> - 「三十個據點、十個國家」如果不確定最新數字，就說 "around thirty locations in about ten countries" — 加 around/about 就不會錯

## M2 — 我的個人故事（約 50 秒｜G 類全部、A、D）

> **這塊最重要，因為可以事先寫死、100% 背熟。**
> 下面是依你的背景寫的草稿 —— **請自己改成完全正確的事實**，然後逐字背下來。

I want to start with something personal.
I was born in Myanmar, and I came to Taiwan to study.
I paid for my own education, so every semester I had to work and study at the same time.

It was not easy. But it gave me two things.
First, **languages**. I can switch between Chinese, English, and my mother tongue.
Second, **patience**. When you work and study together, you learn how to keep going.

That is also why I joined E.SUN.
E.SUN is building a financial platform across Asia,
and Southeast Asia is the part of the world I understand best.
I hope one day I can be the person who connects the two sides.

> **可以接在哪些題目後面**
> - G1 generation gap → 「我跟家人在兩個國家、兩個世代」
> - G6 biggest difficulty → 直接就是這塊
> - D6 international talent → 「我就是這種人才的例子」
> - A2 introduce E.SUN to a foreigner → 「我自己就是外國人，我用外國人聽得懂的方式講」

## M3 — 科技與數位（約 45 秒｜B 類全部、F 部分）

E.SUN puts technology at the center.
We call ourselves **"the bank of technology."**

Let me give you a concrete example. **Fraud detection.**
When your credit card is used somewhere strange,
our AI model checks it **in less than one second** and stops it.
A human being cannot do that. A machine can.

Here is a second number. Our AI reads bill images for us,
and it saves about **seventy-seven percent** of the manual effort on the front line.

We use AI in five areas:
**service, marketing, risk control, process, and employee support.**
So AI is not one product. It is a **layer** under everything we do.

But I want to add one thing.
Technology does not replace people. It **gives time back** to people.
The machine handles the repeated work, so we can spend our time on the customer.

> **中文提醒**
> - 「less than one second」和「77%」兩個數字都出自學長姐的答案（`新人訓.xlsx`），可以放心講
> - "seventy-seven percent" 講出來比寫 77% 好聽，也不會唸錯
> - 最後那句 "gives time back to people" 是很好的收尾，評審會喜歡

## M4 — ESG 與永續（約 45 秒｜C 類全部、A 部分）

E.SUN takes climate change seriously, and we put dates on it.

**By 2030**, all our locations in Taiwan will use **100% renewable energy**.
**By 2035**, we will fully exit coal-related business.
**By 2050**, we will be a **net zero** bank.

We also use lending as a tool.
Through **green loans** and **sustainability-linked loans**,
we give companies a better rate when they hit their environmental targets.
So we are not only reducing our own carbon. We are helping our customers reduce theirs.

And there is the **Golden Seed Project** — we build libraries for children in remote areas.
Because sustainability is not only about the environment. It is also about people.

> **中文提醒**
> - "net zero" 兩個字分開唸，不要黏成 "netzero"
> - "renewable" 唸 /rɪˈnjuːəbl/，重音在第二音節
> - 三個年份是這塊的骨架，忘記內容就先把 2030 / 2035 / 2050 講出來，內容自然會回來

## M5 — 團隊與風險（約 45 秒｜D、E 類全部）

At E.SUN we have a saying:
**"There is no individual hero. There is only a hero team."**

I saw this during the training program.
In the team-building activity, the fastest person did not win.
The team that waited for each other finished first.

The same idea applies to risk.
We have **three lines of defense**.
The first line is the business unit. The second line is risk and compliance.
The third line is internal audit.
Every one of us is the first line. Risk management is not another department's job — it is mine.

And that is why we say: **no business beyond risk, no service beyond regulation.**

> **中文提醒**
> - "There is no individual hero, there is only a hero team" 這句很有力，練到可以不假思索講出來
> - 三道防線英文：first line = business unit / second line = risk & compliance / third line = internal audit

## M6 — 時事一句話（約 30 秒｜F 類全部）

> 這塊**每週要更新一次**。上台前一天早上再看一次晨報。

**Interest rates.**
When the Fed raises rates, borrowing becomes expensive.
Companies invest less, people spend less, and the economy cools down.
For a bank, higher rates can mean a better margin — but also more credit risk.
So it cuts both ways.

**Supply chains.**
Many Taiwanese companies are moving production to Southeast Asia.
That is exactly where E.SUN has built its network.
Their move is our opportunity.

**Taiwan's strength.**
TSMC shows what Taiwan does well — being reliable, year after year.
Reliability is also what a bank sells.

> **中文提醒**
> - "It cuts both ways." = 有好有壞。短、好用、聽起來很道地
> - 最後那句 "Reliability is also what a bank sells" 可以當萬用收尾

## M7 — 藝術與音樂（約 45 秒｜A1、A7、A11，也可當任何題目的 Hook）

> 玉山**每年都考藝術題**，而且是董事長場。完整素材見 `05-ammo-english.md` 第一節。
> 這裡是壓縮版，先背這塊。

In our head office there is a sculpture called **"The Power of Music."**
A French artist named **Arman** made it in 1986.

He took about **twenty double basses**, cut them apart, and stacked them into one piece.
One double bass alone is just an instrument.
Twenty of them, put together, become something you cannot look away from.

That is why I think this work represents E.SUN.
**One plus one becomes more than two.** That is teamwork.
The branch alone cannot serve the customer. The back office alone cannot either.
Only together do we deliver the whole service.

And there is one more piece I like — **the Jade Mountain Deer.**
It was the **first artwork E.SUN ever collected.**
Deer are social animals. They survive together, not alone.
So even the very first thing we bought was already saying: *this is a team.*

A bank is usually a place where people talk about numbers.
Our head office is also a place where people stop and look at something beautiful.
I think that says something about what kind of bank we want to be.

> **中文提醒**
> - "Arman" 唸 /ɑːrˈmɑːn/，法國人名，重音在後
> - "double bass" 唸 /ˈdʌbl beɪs/ — bass 唸「貝斯」不是「巴斯」
> - 「20 把」不確定就說 "about twenty"
> - 這塊也可以整個換成雷諾瓦（見 `05-ammo-english.md`），二選一背熟就好，不要兩個都半生不熟

## 拼裝範例

| 抽到的題目 | 拼法 |
|---|---|
| A2 向外國人介紹玉山 | Hook → **M1** → **M4**（COP 場合講 ESG）→ **M2**（我是外國人，我懂）→ Close |
| B4 ChatGPT 對銀行的影響 | Hook → **M3** → Counterpoint（幻覺、資安）→ **M5**（所以要有三道防線）→ Close |
| C1 氣候變遷玉山能做什麼 | Hook → **M4** → **M1**（責任是我們的核心價值）→ Close |
| D1 團隊致勝關鍵 | Hook → **M5** → **M2**（我自己的例子）→ Close |
| G6 最大的困難 | Hook → **M2** 整塊展開 → Close |
| F1 Fed 升息影響 | Hook → **M6** → **M5**（風險管理）→ Close |
| A1 哪位藝術家最打動你 | Hook → **M7** 整塊展開 → **M5**（團隊，因為阿曼講的就是團隊）→ Close |
| A7 從藝術品學到什麼 | Hook → **M7** → **M1**（這就是玉山的樣子）→ Close |
| A11 雷諾瓦「痛苦會過去」 | Hook → **M7 雷諾瓦版** → **M2**（我自己的痛苦）→ Close |
